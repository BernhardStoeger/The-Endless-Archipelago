
- Alignment: Lawful Neutral

- Symbol: A gilded anchor with crossed swords.
    
- Base of Operations: The capital city of Veldros, located on a massive island called the Sovereign’s Spire. This island is dominated by a towering citadel where the High Marshal resides.
    
- Description: The Iron Fleet is the primary enforcer of law and order across the archipelago. They operate like a naval police force, collecting tributes from islands in exchange for protection from raiders, rebellions, and monsters. Their presence is both a shield and a shackle—islands that defy their rule risk being left vulnerable to pirates, storms, or worse.

    - Methods: They use large ships to patrol the waters and maintain order. Their soldiers are well-trained, heavily armored, and armed with standard weapons.

    - Corruption: While they claim to enforce justice, their heavy-handed tactics often lead to cruelty. Some islands pay tribute under threat, and the Iron Fleet’s Black Brigades (elite enforcers) are known for torturing dissenters.

    - Weakness: Their bureaucracy is slow to respond to local disputes, and their rule is resented by many. They also rely on proprietary navigation charts, which only they possess, making travel to remote islands difficult without their help.


- Player Interaction: If the players have ties to an Iron Fleet-occupied island, they might be recruited to root out rebellion or smuggling. Alternatively, they could be accused of crimes by the Iron Fleet (setting up a quest to clear their name or escape the law).
