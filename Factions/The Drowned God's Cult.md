
- Alignment: Neutral Evil

- Symbol: A drowned, skeletal hand clutching a trident, often found on cave walls or buried in tidal pools.
    
- Base of Operations: The Sunken Cathedral, a submerged temple complex near the Abyssal Maw, a whirlpool that leads to the depths. Their visible strongholds are on islands with black sands (where the ground is littered with petrified bones).
    
- Description: The cult worships Kraken’s Maw, a god-like entity sealed away after the Great Drowning (a cataclysmic flood that created most of the archipelago’s islands). They believe that if they awaken Kraken’s Maw, the world will be "purified" by drowning—with the faithful rising as Drowned Ones (undead servants) to rule the new watery world.
    
    - Methods:
        - They use necromancy to raise drowned sailors as husks.
        - They poison wells and sabotage ships to drown victims, creating more sacrifices.
        - They’ve infiltrated the Iron Fleet, planting agents who ensure that "unworthy" sailors are executed in ways that drown them.
    - Weakness: Their rituals require specific artifacts (e.g., the Crown of Tides, a relic from the Great Drowning) and blood sacrifices. Their numbers are small, but their influence is growing.
    
- Player Interaction: The players might stumble upon a drowned village, uncover a hidden ritual site, or be hired to stop a cultist from sinking a ship. The cult’s presence is subtle at first—missing sailors, strange tides, or fishermen who return... wrong.
