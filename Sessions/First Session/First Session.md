
### Start in the Tavern 

**Island & Village Background**
Driftwood Isle is a small archipelago island with scattered villages. The primary settlement is **Harbor's Rest**, a bustling port town with a central pier, fishmongers, and a single market square. Smaller villages like **Saltmarsh** (fishing-focused) and **Cedar Point** (forestry) dot the coastline. Harbor's Rest is the only place with a reliable port, making it a hub for sailors and traders. *The Iron Fleet enforces tribute here, using heavy-handed tactics that fuel resentment among locals* [4].


Players start in the Tavern each of them searching for he opportunity to set sail with a captain. Each player has to come with his reason on his own. 

**Tavern Details**
**The Salty Dog Tavern** (located in Harbor's Rest) serves as the starting hub.
- **Barkeeper**: *Brin* (a former sailor missing a leg, wary of Iron Fleet patrols).
- **Atmosphere**: Smells of salt, ale, and fish. A notice board lists crew jobs (e.g., "Sailor needed for coastal run").
- **Key Hook**: Brin whispers, *"The Iron Fleet’s Black Brigades took a fisherman’s daughter for ‘stealing nets’—they’re holding her at the docks. If you can get her back, I’ll recommend you to a good captain."* [4]

**Side Quest: "The Missing Daughter"**  
_Duration: ~2 hours_

1. **Setup**: Players learn of Kael (fisherman) and his daughter, Lyra, taken by Iron Fleet sailors. The dockside camp (2-4 sailors) is active—_sailors rotate patrols, creating variable detection chances_.
    
2. **Optional Encounter (Dynamic Resolution)**:
    
    - _Location_: Iron Fleet dockside camp (2 sailors guarding Lyra; 1 additional sailor patrolling nearby crates).
    - _Dynamic Options_:
        - **Stealth**: Players must succeed on a DC 12 Perception/Stealth check _during a 30-second window_ (sailors are distracted by a loud argument over stolen fish). _Fail_ → Combat begins (2 sailors).
        - **Distract**: Players can trigger a crate explosion (DC 10 Thievery) to create chaos, avoiding combat entirely.
        - **Negotiate**: Players use Kael’s fishing net (found earlier) as a bribe (DC 10 Persuasion), paying sailors to let Lyra go.
    - _Outcome_: If combat occurs, sailors act aggressively (e.g., shouting for help), escalating tension.
3. **Primary Encounter (Variable Ambush)**:
    
    - _After retrieving Lyra_, Iron Fleet sailors (2-4 members of the Iron Fleet soldiers) ambush players _based on prior actions_:
        - _If players caused noise_ (e.g., crate explosion): 4 sailors arrive.
        - _If players avoided detection_: 2 sailors arrive, but their leader (a veteran) taunts: _"You think you’re safe? The Iron Fleet owns this dock."_
    - _Dynamic Resolution_:
        - **Environmental Tactics**: Players can use the dock’s unstable crates (DC 12 Athletics to topple) to knock sailors off balance.
        - **Captain Elias’s Arrival**: _Only if players are losing_ (e.g., below 50% HP) does Elias intervene. His line: _"The captain’s voice cuts through the chaos: ‘You’re with me now. The Iron Fleet won’t have you.’"_ First Session.md
        - _If players win quickly_: Elias arrives earlier, offering a recruitment pitch instead of combat.

**After coming back sailors of [[The Iron Fleet]] pick a fight with them. The players most likely will win against the sailors but then the leader of the sailors comes in: [[Lieutenant Rourke Steel]]. He is way to strong for them. Just as they are beaten a mysterious captains comes in and saves them. It is [[Captain Elias Veyne]] and he is searching for a crew.**

Conversation between Rourke Steel and Captain Elias:
- Elias: Long time no see Rourke name of leader.
- Rourke: Veyne!
- Elias: Didn't i tell you last time you should not make up charges as an excuse to kidnap girls? What is it this time stolen fish?
- Rourke: I don't make up charges and she stole fishing nets!
- Elias: Fishing Nets? Really? You should come up with something better. Nobody would believe that you are important enough to go after net thieves. Let the girl go, walk away now, or we'll settle this properly - and you know I'll win."
- Rourke (glancing at his men, grinding his teeth): "Fine. But don't think this is over, Veyne."

**Feedback & Recommendations**
1. **Tie Side Quest to Iron Fleet Corruption**: The quest directly references their abuse of power (e.g., false charges for tribute) [4].
2. **Captain Elias’s Introduction**: His arrival during the fight creates urgency for players to join his crew [2]. Add a line: *"The captain’s voice cuts through the chaos: ‘You’re with me now. The Iron Fleet won’t have you.’"*
