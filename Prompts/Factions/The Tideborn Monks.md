
- Alignment: Lawful Good

- Symbol: A lotus flower floating on water.

- Base: The Veil Islands, a chain of mist-shrouded islands where the monks meditate in giant floating stupas.

- Description: Ascetics who believe the sea is a cycle of birth and death. They heal drowning victims (using magic to revive them) and warn of coming storms (via their "Tide Sight" powers). They despise the Drowned God’s Cult but won’t fight them directly.

- Interaction: The players might seek their help to raise a drowned ally or learn about the Cult’s plans.
