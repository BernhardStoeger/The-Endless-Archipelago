I would like to create a new dnd campaign. The world should be similar to one piece, a planet with a lot of water and a lot of islands of different sizes. Except for a few exceptions, the islands should be fixed and not float around.  

The group starts at a small island. They all have their own personal reason why they want to sail and explore the world.

In the first session i want to place them in an inn, where they get involved in an fight with a group responsible for law enforcement. After winning, by beating the others up. The leader of the beat up guys comes in. He is way to strong for them. Just as they are beaten a mysterious captains comes in and saves them. 

This captain searches for a crew and just arrived. He will be a DM NPC who is strong but not overpowered. Must of the time he will stay near the ship and will not fight. We will have to create a reason why he does so. Also we need a backstory for him. He has his own ship which from here on is the base for the party.
