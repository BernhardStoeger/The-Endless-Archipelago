# Aris Thorne, First Mate of Captain Elias Veyne

## Role
Former First Mate of *The Marrow's Grace*, now a Tideborn Monk working to help Captain Elias navigate his bond with the ship.

## Background
Served as Elias Veyne's first mate for five years before the crew's departure. A pragmatic but fiercely loyal navigator who saw Elias's bond with the ship as a shared burden, not a curse. The only crew member who consistently advocated for Elias during his early struggles with the ship's bond.

## Key Details

- **The Logbook Entry**: Wrote the crew's final logbook entry: *"We left to save ourselves, but I should have stayed. Elias was not the monster they feared—he is the ship's heart"
- **Crew Misunderstanding**: Tried to warn the crew about the Drowned God's Cult's trap during the Iron Fleet ambush but was overruled. His warnings were dismissed as "Elias's madness."
- **Redemption Arc**: Now a Tideborn Monk, working secretly with the *Tideborn Monks* to help Elias navigate his bond with the ship. Leaves subtle clues for the new crew, such as a tide-locked journal entry in navigational charts: *"He saved us all. We were the ones who failed."
