**Skills** Athletics +6, Intimidation +3, Perception +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge Rating** 6 (2,300 XP)

**Legendary Resistance** (3/Day). If Rourke fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  

**Iron Fleet Authority**. As a bonus action, Rourke can issue a command to one Iron Fleet sailor within 30 feet. The sailor must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute and follow Rourke's commands. This can be used up to three times per day.

**Tactics**: Rourke prefers intimidation over direct combat. He uses his authority to control his men while engaging opponents with his heavy hammer. He'll only engage in direct combat when necessary, often trying to force players to surrender rather than fight.


**Actions**  

**Multiattack**. Rourke makes three melee attacks.  

**Heavy Hammer**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit_: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.  

**Command**. Rourke issues a command to one target he can see within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute. While charmed, the target must follow Rourke's commands to the best of its ability.

**Reactions**  
**Parry**. When Rourke is hit by a melee attack, he can use his reaction to reduce the damage by 6 (1d12).

_Note: Rourke has a reputation for crushing mutinies with his bare hands First Session.md, making him a formidable leader who relies on intimidation rather than brute force alone._
