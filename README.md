# The-Endless-Archipelago


# Premise

- Longer running DnD campaign for four Players and one DM
-  The world is similar to one piece, a planet with a lot of water and a lot of islands of different sizes. Except for a few exceptions, the islands should be fixed and not float around.  


# Beginning

The group starts at a small island. They all have their own personal reason why they want to sail and explore the world.

[[Captain Elias Veyne]] searches for a crew and just arrived. He will be a DM NPC who is strong but not overpowered. He has his own ship which from here on is the base for the party. Other than him there is no other crew.
